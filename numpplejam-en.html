NUMPLE JAM



📋 Game Rules

  • NUMPLE JAM is a game where two players use 5 number cards and 4 operator cards to create expressions as close as possible to the target number.
  • The player whose expression is closer to the target number receives 10 points.
  • Set the target number and start the game.
  • You don't have to use all the cards.
  • You can place two cards together to make a two-digit number.
  • Numple Jam uses addition (+), subtraction (-), and multiplication (x) operations.
  • Suitable for students in Grade 3 or above who have learned multiplication tables.
  • Arrange various numbers and operation symbols strategically to get close to the target number.
  • In expressions, multiplication is calculated before addition or subtraction.
